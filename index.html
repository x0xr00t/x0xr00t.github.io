Research Principles

Authorization & Scope

Testing should be performed with explicit permission and within the agreed scope, with care taken to minimize disruption and unintended impact.

Evidence First

Findings should be supported by reproducible observations, clear confidence levels, and a distinction between confirmed issues and hypotheses.

Responsible Reporting

When a vulnerability affects a third party, coordinate disclosure appropriately and provide enough technical detail to support remediation.

Defensive Outcomes

The objective is to help organizations understand risk, strengthen controls, and verify that fixes address the underlying cause.
